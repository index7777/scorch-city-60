## Intelligence records — 100 planning slots

Route/location (24): I-R01..I-R24 cover witnessed roads, blocked passages, entrances, local shortcuts, access points, and observed silhouettes. No record names an unseen destination.

Resource/stock (20): I-S01..I-S20 cover observed finite stock, moved stock, exhausted sources, damaged stock, and credible/uncertain reports of supplies.

People/social (20): I-P01..I-P20 cover personally met people, names learned from others, relationship claims, last-seen reports, requests, conflicts, and group movements.

Hazard/infrastructure (20): I-H01..I-H20 cover directly observed heat hazards, power behavior, cooling behavior, structural hazards, equipment states, and local infrastructure reports.

Historical/causal (16): I-C01..I-C16 cover logs, testimony, manifests, maintenance history and event evidence that may later support deductions.

Every intelligence slot must carry source, reliability, observed time, verification state and stale/expiry behavior before implementation.

## Ending-route architecture — 4 hidden routes

Internal codenames only; player-facing identity must emerge from discoveries.

- `ending-route-alpha`: restore a large-scale long-term survival capability through infrastructure repair and sustained operation.
- `ending-route-beta`: establish a distributed, maintainable survival network rather than relying on one central system.
- `ending-route-gamma`: organize a physically credible relocation/evacuation chain using people, transport and finite supplies.
- `ending-route-delta`: build a stable local community capable of indefinite survival through logistics, division of labor and protected resources.

None is a Day-60 deadline. None appears in UI until deductions and material capability make it knowable.

## Dependency spine

Exploration observation → intelligence → conversation topic / verification action → clue → deduction → project possibility → people/skill/resource assembly → execution events → resolution.

NPC knowledge can shortcut or redirect parts of the chain, but cannot reveal facts the NPC does not actually know. False or stale intelligence may create detours without creating hidden-world UI leakage.
